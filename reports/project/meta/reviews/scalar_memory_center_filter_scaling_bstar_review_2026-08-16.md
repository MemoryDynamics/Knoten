## 2. The M0 intervention was active

The near-zero \(M_0\) mass exponent is not caused by an accidentally inert
parameter. Eta was fixed rather than rematched. At
\(\tau=\mu=0.5\), changing \(M_0\) from 0.5 to 2 changes the local
\(\kappa\) from approximately 2 to 8 and the measured discrete damping from
approximately 3.98 to 9.92. The inferred inertial coefficient remains one,
as the filter architecture predicts.

This separation is the most discriminating part of B-star: the intervention
strongly changes one fitted impedance coefficient while leaving the other
independent of stored load.

## 4. Why the pass is nearly exact

The generic OLS estimator does not receive \(\tau\), \(\mu\) or \(M_0\), so
the numerical coefficient recovery is not hard-coded into the fit. However,
the target data are generated by the same declared architecture whose local
untruncated equation implies

\[
v_n=a v_{n-1}+bF_n,
\qquad
b={h\mu\over\tau}.
\]

The impulses are deliberately weak, mirrored branches cancel common noise,
the radii remain deep in the Taylor regime and the finite tail is only about
\(6\,10^{-6}\). Consequently the numerical experiment measures an almost
ideal local derivative. Its precision is therefore expected and should not
be presented as multiple independent discoveries of mass.

What the run adds beyond algebra is narrower but useful: the full nonlinear
ring-buffer implementation, finite tail, reweighting, M0-dependent force,
procedural formation and external force normalization all preserve the
predicted coefficient across an intervention panel and an unseen joint
combination.

## 5. Remaining alternative explanations and limitations

- The holdout is an unseen factorial combination, not a new parameter level
  or an extrapolation beyond the registered range.
- The three fixed rivals are useful falsifiers but do not exhaust all
  possible material or active-medium models.
- Common random numbers and mirrored derivatives are variance controls.
  Seed rows are not independent experimental replications supporting a
  population-level p-value.
- The panel fixes \(h=0.005\) and tail extent 12. B-star is not a uniform
  small-step or infinite-memory theorem.
- Weak local response does not test whether the same scaling survives a
  nonlinear large-amplitude regime or a formation transition.
- A normalized occupancy-history centroid may have an inertial transfer
  coefficient without being the center of conserved matter.
- The effective reciprocal wrapper was engineered at A2. B-star does not
  select it as the natural microscopic K0 actuator.

Physical B, C, physical-work E and additive-mass F1 remain closed. Resuming
