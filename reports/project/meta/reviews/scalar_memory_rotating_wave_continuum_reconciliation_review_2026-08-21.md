## 6. Remaining referee objections

Several limitations remain material:

- The continuum target is not interval certified. All three quadrature panels
  use double precision; two share NumPy's Gauss--Legendre implementation and
  the third supplies an independent SciPy implementation. Their agreement is
  strong numerical evidence, not a theorem bounding quadrature truncation and
  floating-point error.
- Newton plus a branch corridor finds one local root. It does not establish
  global uniqueness or exclude other continuum branches.
- The reconciliation reuses the five revealed ladder cells. It repairs a
  target-definition error but supplies no new finite-$\alpha$ holdout.
- Local uniqueness of each balance root is not dynamical stability. Only the
  $\alpha=0.01$ anchor has passed the full-FIFO stability gate.
- A prepared rotating history is not generic formation. Basin size, spontaneous
  chirality selection, noise survival and parameter robustness remain open.
- The orbit is an ambient spatial $SO(2)$ group orbit. Quotienting that symmetry
  leaves a point, so no additional internal $S^1$ coordinate has been shown.
- The memory write/forget process is dissipative and open. Neither the circle
  nor the continuum scaling supplies a force-work balance, conserved momentum,
  inertial law or physical mass.

The strongest next discriminating check is not another fitted continuum
target. It is a prospectively sealed new refinement cell, for example
$\alpha=0.00125$, $H=9600$, $\eta=0.01875$, evaluated against the now fixed
continuum target without changing any tolerance. Separately, a non-anchor
full-FIFO stability cell would test whether stability, rather than mere root
existence, persists under refinement. Neither step should open the sealed
$A_{\rm att}=7$ holdout or relabel the ambient orbit as internal topology.
