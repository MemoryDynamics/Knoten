0.0323--0.1082 target radii, compared with the $10^{-7}R_3$ P1 perturbations
and at most $10^{-4}R_3$ P2 forcing. The two target-blind histories begin at
0.6319 and 0.6787 target radii.

The wrong-rate ellipse uses only $\alpha$ and $\sigma_{\rm rep}$ and begins

nonperiodic and also contains neither $R_3$ nor $\theta_3$. Both are at least

These facts rule out the narrow criticism that P3 merely repeated a tiny
prepared-circle perturbation. They do not make the histories generic. Each is
an analytic delay initial condition supplied at update zero, not a history
generated by a previous autonomous trajectory.
