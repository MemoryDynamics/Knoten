## What the Project Examines

Memory Dynamics studies discrete dynamics with relaxing memory.
Project website: [MemoryDynamics.org](https://memorydynamics.org/). Personal
motivation and possible future physical interpretations must be
distinguished from tested model results. The project has not demonstrated
a theory of everything, particles, physical mass, charge, or spin. The
[English article draft](https://github.com/MemoryDynamics/Knoten/blob/main/docs/outreach/medium_draft_en.md)
and its
[German article on Medium](https://medium.com/@haukeh/ein-traum-von-knoten-9232412d4ff8)
provide an accessible account. The neural interpretation of the dream and
possible usefulness for AI remain hypotheses; the model findings do not
establish brain functions or learning ability.

You do not have to share the underlying idea or read the entire repository.
One verifiable criticism or reproduction is enough. The source revision
linked below is fixed so that the review question does not change during
your examination. The technical sources are currently in German; equations,
code, and machine-readable records accompany them.

G5 artifact hashes can be checked across platforms. The bit-exact semantic
audit of the historical record has a documented platform boundary. A
corresponding difference needs investigation; it is not automatically a new
scientific error or a pass. Internal reviews and AI-assisted checks do not
replace independent external replication.

## Recording Your Feedback

Use the
[English GitHub review issue template](https://github.com/MemoryDynamics/Knoten/issues/new?template=focused_review_en.md).
It asks for the claim and source revision, method, observed result,
interpretation, and remaining limits. Report negative or incomplete results
as well. An unchecked claim has not passed a test.

Keep a contribution focused on one question where possible. Once that
question is addressed, the contribution can be considered complete; there
is no expectation that you take on the whole project. Proposals for new
experiments remain proposals until incorporated into the existing project
governance.
